Tourist Places

Explore the most breathtaking destinations and must-visit landmarks.

Putri Nglirip Waterfall

Putri Nglirip Waterfall is a natural tourist icon of Tuban, famous for the beauty of its turquoise-green water. Located in Singgahan Subdistrict, this destination is very popular among tourists who want to enjoy the cool, rural atmosphere surrounded by lush trees along the waterfall’s stream.

Boom Beach

Boom Beach is a legendary stretch of coastline strategically located near Tuban’s Town Square. This beach is not merely a natural recreational destination; it also has a rich historical significance, as it was once a vital ancient port.

View details →

Kelapa Tuban Beach

If you’re looking for a shady coastal setting, Kelapa Tuban Beach is the perfect choice and has been a popular destination lately. Unlike the typically scorching heat of Java’s northern coast, this spot offers a very shaded recreational area thanks to the thousands of coconut and palm trees planted in rows along the shoreline.

View details →

Akbar Cave

Another natural attraction that Tuban is proud of is Akbar Cave, a natural masterpiece with a rather unique location—it lies directly beneath the hustle and bustle of the city’s market and residential areas. This massive cave is renowned for the beauty of its magnificent stalactite and stalagmite formations.
