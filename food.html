Local Food

Discover the authentic tastes and hidden culinary gems of my region.

Kari Rajungan

As a coastal region, seafood is the star of the show in Tuban, and Kari Rajungan is its culinary masterpiece. Rajungan (a type of crab) is cooked in a reddish-yellow coconut milk broth that is very thick and rich in spices. The main characteristic of this dish is its intensely spicy and savory flavor. Kari rajungan is best enjoyed with a serving of warm white rice or corn rice.

Becek Menthok

Becek menthok is a dish with a thick, rich broth similar to gulai or curry, but it uses menthok meat (serati duck, a type of duck) as its main ingredient. Menthok meat, which is typically known for being tough, is prepared in such a way that its texture becomes very tender and completely free of any fishy odor. The “becek” sauce combines coconut milk with strong spices such as coriander and cumin, resulting in a blend of savory, fresh, and—of course—spicy flavors.

Rica-Rica Belut

In addition to its seafood, Tuban is also very famous for its rice paddy eel dishes. Tuban’s signature spicy eel—or “rica-rica belut”—is cooked with an abundance of spices and bird’s eye chili peppers, creating an extremely spicy sensation that explodes in your mouth. This dish is typically served with fresh corn rice (sego empok). The combination of the corn’s natural sweetness with the savory spiciness of the eel creates a truly authentic rustic flavor.
